# Dwarfium Docs

Three free applications around the **DWARF II, DWARF 3 and DWARF mini** telescopes. They work on their own, and better together.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-rocket-launch:{ .lg .middle } **Dwarfium Hub**

    ---

    Installs, updates and starts the other two. Keeps your Dwarfs and your installations (home PC, laptop, mini PC) in step.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Discover the Hub](apps/hub.md)

-   :material-telescope:{ .lg .middle } **Dwarfium Lite**

    ---

    Drives your Dwarfs: live control, programs, scheduling, the Dwarf's own schedule, mosaics, end of the night. From a PC, a phone or a tablet.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Discover Dwarfium Lite](apps/lite.md)

-   :material-archive-star:{ .lg .middle } **Dwarfium Scope Archive**

    ---

    Backs up, organises, explores and processes your sessions. Tells you what to shoot tonight.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Discover Scope Archive](apps/scope-archive.md)

</div>

!!! tip "Search tips"
    The search looks into every page. The docs are in English, like the apps' default language: search with the English word (*mosaic*, not *mosaïque*).
    Search for **what you want to do** (`archive`, `power off`, `wake up`, `mosaic`) or **for a word you see on screen** (`Check`, `Force resume`, `Tonight's plan`).
