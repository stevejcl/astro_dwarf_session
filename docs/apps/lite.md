# Dwarfium Lite

**Drives your DWARF II, DWARF 3 and DWARF mini** — several at once — from a PC window, a browser, a phone or a tablet. Also known by its repository name, *Astro Dwarf Session*.

[Releases](https://github.com/stevejcl/astro_dwarf_session/releases){ .md-button .md-button--primary } [Source](https://github.com/stevejcl/astro_dwarf_session){ .md-button }

## Dashboard (home page)

One card per Dwarf: connected or not, battery, sensor temperature, free space, the target being shot, capture progress (captured / requested, stacked, end time), a live thumbnail, the last program's result. **Connect all**; reorder or hide cards.
→ [Connect and manage Dwarfs](../how-to/connect.md)
